# Future Enhancement Plan - Budget Manager Screen

Here is the compiled list of premium suggestions and feature plans to further improve the **Budget Manager** screen:

---

## 1. Category Sub-Limits (Granular Control) 📊
Instead of just a single monthly limit, set specific budgets for individual categories.
*   **Behavior**: Set budgets like *Leisure limit: ₹5,000* or *Food limit: ₹8,000*.
*   **UI Experience**: Category chips or list headers will glow or turn amber/red when their individual sub-limit is breached.

## 2. Gamified Savings Streaks & Achievements 🏆
Add a "Milestones" section on the overview screen to reward good financial habits.
*   **Proposed Badges**:
    *   *“Savings Streak: 3 Months under budget!”*
    *   *“Frugal Master: Spent less than ₹2,000 on Leisure this month.”*
    *   *“Zero Deficit Hero: All months in surplus.”*
*   **UI Experience**: Retro/modern neon glowing badges that unlock as the user reaches milestones.

## 3. Month-on-Month Comparison Trends 📈
Add a quick comparison indicator on the Month Details screen showing how your spending compares to the previous month.
*   **Behavior**: *"You've spent ₹2,100 less on Food compared to last month at this date."* or *"Transport spending is 20% higher than average."*

## 4. Interactive Savings Goals (Piggy Banks) 🐷
Add a goals planner where you can set targets for specific purchases.
*   **Behavior**: Set targets like *"Buy New GPU: Target ₹45,000 (Saved ₹15,000)"*.
*   **Integration**: When a month has surplus savings (limit - spent), tap a button to allocate that surplus directly to one of your savings goals.

## 5. Bill Reminders & Calendar Timeline 📅
A compact calendar timeline showing when recurring template bills are typically due.
*   **Behavior**: Tag templates with a due day (e.g. *Rent: Day 5, Broadband: Day 10*).
*   **UI Experience**: A visual checklist of pending bills for the current month so you never miss a payment.

## 6. CSV Data Export / Share 📤
Export your monthly details to a clean CSV file or copy it to the clipboard as a formatted text summary.
